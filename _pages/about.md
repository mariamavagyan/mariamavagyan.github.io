**I am on the job market for ML scientist, ML engineer, and research scientist roles starting spring/summer 2027.**

I am a 5th year PhD Candidate in Electrical Engineering at Columbia University, advised by [Professor John Wright](https://www.engineering.columbia.edu/faculty-staff/directory/john-wright). My research develops geometry-aware machine learning algorithms, with applications to [gravitational waves](https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/gravitational-waves/en/), as well as to natural and medical images. I'm particularly interested in provable, reliable methods for noisy scientific data, from manifold denoising for diffusion models to white-box networks for detecting weak signals.

During my PhD, I have had the honor of collaborating with [Professor Szabolcs Márka](https://www.physics.columbia.edu/content/szabolcs-marka), [Dr. Zsuzsa Márka](https://zsuzsamarka.org/), and [Professor Shweta Mahajan](https://s-mahajan.github.io/).

I am a recipient of the 2025 [Qualcomm Innovation Fellowship](https://www.qualcomm.com/research/university-relations/innovation-fellowship/2025-north-america).

I've also worked in industry: as a Research Scientist Intern at IBM, I compared transformer-based, state-space, and hybrid large language models for tool calling. As a Graduate Research Intern at Amgen, I used machine learning on clinical data to predict serious side effects of cancer therapies.

Outside of research, I enjoy cycling, yoga, knitting, and pickling vegetables. I've spoken at UCLA's Promise Armenian Institute, written about gender issues in Armenia, and done non-profit work for over 10 years.
