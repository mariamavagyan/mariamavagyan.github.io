I am a 5th year PhD Candidate in Electrical Engineering at Columbia University, advised by [Professor John Wright](https://www.engineering.columbia.edu/faculty-staff/directory/john-wright). I have had the honor of collaborating with [Professor Szabolcs Márka](https://www.physics.columbia.edu/content/szabolcs-marka), [Dr. Zsuzsa Marka](https://zsuzsamarka.org/), and [Professor Shweta Mahajan](https://s-mahajan.github.io/). My research is on geometry-aware machine learning algorithms with wide applications including but not limited to [gravitational waves](https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/gravitational-waves/en/), natural and medical images.
