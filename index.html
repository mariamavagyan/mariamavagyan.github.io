Mariam Avagyan

PhD Candidate

I am on the job market for AI Research Scientist and Machine Learning engineer roles starting spring/summer 2027.

I am a 5th year PhD Candidate in Electrical Engineering at Columbia University, advised by Professor John Wright. My research develops geometry-aware machine learning algorithms, with applications to gravitational waves, as well as to natural and medical images. I’m particularly interested in provable, reliable methods for noisy scientific data, from manifold denoising for diffusion models to white-box networks for detecting weak signals.

During my PhD, I have had the honor of collaborating with Professor Szabolcs Márka, Dr. Zsuzsa Márka, and Professor Shweta Mahajan.

I am a recipient of the 2025 Qualcomm Innovation Fellowship.

I’ve also worked in industry: as a Research Scientist Intern at IBM, I compared transformer-based, state-space, and hybrid large language models for tool calling. As a Graduate Research Intern at Amgen, I used machine learning on clinical data to predict serious side effects of cancer therapies.

Outside of research, I enjoy cycling, yoga, knitting, and pickling vegetables. I’ve spoken at UCLA’s Promise Armenian Institute, written about gender issues in Armenia, and done non-profit work for over 10 years.
